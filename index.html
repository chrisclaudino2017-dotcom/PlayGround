MILO

He only wants to say happy birthday.

TOUCH · TILT · HEADPHONES RECOMMENDED Contains flashing lights and jump scares.

HOW TO PLAY

ESCAPE

Your car is out of gas and the keys are gone. Search the house, take the red gas can and your keys, then get back to the garage.

MILO

He freezes in your flashlight beam. He does not freeze in the dark. Keep the light on him when he is close — but watch the battery.

CONTROLS

Touch: left thumb to move, right thumb to look. Tap LIGHT, SENSE and USE.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows to move, mouse to look, F light, E use, Q sense, Esc pause.

SENSE

Tap SENSE to feel where the next objective is. It costs a little battery. Batteries are scattered around the house.
